# 03 — Storyline (AAA Campaign)

> Critical path: **~12 hours**. Mission IDs (e.g. `M1.03`) are shared by writing, design, level art, audio and the data files in [13 — Technical Architecture](13-technical-architecture.md). Detailed mission design docs for flagship missions are in [18 — Mission Design Documents](18-mission-design-documents.md); screenplay samples are in [20 — Script Samples](20-script-samples.md).

> **Open world first.** After the 30-minute prologue, every act is an **open region you can explore freely** (see [08 — Open World & Exploration](08-open-world-and-exploration.md)). Missions are **Leads** in your journal that you pick up in any order, at your own pace. The timestamps below are the **median time a player following the story** reaches each beat; players who explore will take much longer, and that's the point. Beats marked *systemic* fire from world state at a safe moment, not on a timer.

---

## 3.1 The story in one paragraph

Three years ago, you steered the robotic arm that took a sample from an interstellar object called Styx. Tonight, that sample gets out of its lab beneath your spaceport, and the dead begin to rise. Your older sister, Ada, a Directorate pilot, promises to hold a seat for you on the last shuttle off Earth — and then leaves you behind on her commander's orders. You fortify a hangar, rescue a girl who can't be infected, and build your own way to orbit. Up there you turn a dead station into a home, and learn the plague is not a disease but a **gardener**: an ancient alien machine built to terraform worlds. Earth was one of its gardens once, 252 million years ago — the Great Dying was its first harvest. Now its Seedship is waking to finish the job, and the man who abandoned Earth wants to use the girl you saved to control it. To save your family, you'll have to cross the stars, face your sister, and decide what should grow on Earth next.

**Logline:** *A spaceport engineer who helped bring a plague to Earth must build a way off-world, protect a found family, and face the sister who left them behind — before the alien gardener that made the dead returns to finish its harvest.*

**Tagline:** *"We brought it home. Now we build our way out."*

## 3.2 The three strands

AAA stories work when the spectacle is carried by something personal. EXODUS PROTOCOL braids three strands; every chapter advances at least two.

| Strand | Question | Carried by | Resolved in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — The Garden** (mystery / spectacle) | *What is the Verdance, and what does it want?* | Mara, KESTREL, Sable, the Sowers' ruins | M4.05 — the Loom |
| **B — The Hearth** (found family / colony) | *Can we still be a people worth saving?* | Lily, Tug, Idris, the colony sim | M3.06 & M4.06 — the Sunday dinners |
| **C — Blood** (sibling drama / antagonist) | *Can you forgive the person who left you?* | Ada Okafor, Director Crane | M4.03 — "Blood" |

**Theme:** *What do we owe each other at the end of the world?*
**Thematic engine:** everyone is a kind of **gardener**. The Sowers *seed and harvest*. Crane *prunes* (he keeps a bonsai on his desk). Mother Sable *surrenders to the garden*. The Engineer *builds and tends*. The four endings are the four philosophies.

## 3.5 Cold Open — "Contact" (0:00–0:03)

**Kestrel Mission Control — November 2068.** Playable. A cramped control room, CRT-warm monitors, coffee cups. You are a junior engineer at the **Persephone arm console**, remote-operating a robotic drill arm 2.1 billion kilometers away on the surface of **Styx**.

1. KESTREL counts down the communications delay. You guide the drill (simple two-stick control — the first thing the player ever does is *build/operate a machine*).
2. The drill bites. Telemetry spikes. On the grainy camera, the surface of Styx **ripples** — like skin flinching.
3. The sample chamber seals. The room erupts in cheers. **Dr. Mara Voss**, at the back, quietly: *"…Did it just move?"* Nobody hears her.
4. A hand on your shoulder: **Director Halvard Crane**, smiling. *"Congratulations. You just touched history."*
5. Smash cut to black. Five soft radio pulses. **"THREE YEARS LATER."**

> **Why it matters:** the player's own hands brought the Verdance home. The guilt is theirs as much as Mara's or Crane's — and it's why this story is personal from the first minute.

## 3.6 Prologue — "Night Zero" (0:03–0:30)

### `M0.01` — "Last Shift" (0:03–0:10)
**Kestrel Aerospace Complex, Nevada. March 24, 2071, 21:40.**
- You are **the Engineer** — everyone calls you **"Wrench."** Late shift in Hangar 4, tuning the guidance computer of the crew vehicle ***Wren***.
- **Tutorial:** movement; multitool weld/grind/scan; fix a panel; carry and install a part.
- **Video call from Ada** on your wrist pad. She's in a Directorate flight suit at the Ark hangar across the complex. Sibling banter — Sunday dinner, Mom's jollof, who burns it. She mentions they've had her crew on standby all week, "fueling everything. Don't ask." (Foreshadow.)
- **Tug Brennan** arrives with two coffees and a bad joke. A security guard, **Dana Marsh**, waves as she passes the hangar window: *"Night, Wrench."*
- The lights flicker. KESTREL: *"Code Black. Biological. All personnel shelter in place."* Blast doors slam.

### `M0.02` — "Code Black" (0:10–0:20)
- Tug went to the parts cage before lockdown. His radio: a scream, then static.
- Dark service corridors. Emergency strobes. **Your only weapon is Ada's wrench.**
- **First Hollow:** Dana Marsh. Same badge, same ponytail, head tilted wrong. She knocks on the glass three times before she comes through it.
- **Tutorial:** stealth (noise meter, crouch, light), melee. You're **scratched** — the Infection HUD crawls in green from the screen edges. KESTREL guides you to a med cabinet: **first Suppressant**.
- You find Tug pinned under a toppled parts rack with a Hollow clawing at him. **Physics rescue:** drive the forklift to lift the rack (no QTEs — machines solve problems).

### `M0.03` — "The Promise" (0:20–0:30) — *Inciting incident*
- Ada calls, breathless: *"Pad 12. I'm holding a seat for you. Run."*
- **Set piece — Terminal B:** sprint through the evacuation as it collapses. A crowd pressing at gates; Hollows *inside* the crowd; a skywalk shattering under the weight of people; a fuel truck exploding in the drop-off lane. Tug limping, you half-carrying him.
- **Pad 12:** Ada's shuttle ***Seraph*** on the pad, ramp down. Directorate troopers at the fence holding the crowd back with rifles. Order on the open channel: *"No unscreened personnel."*
- Ada argues on the radio. **Crane's voice**, calm: *"Commander Okafor. Lift off, or I will find someone who will."*
- The ramp rises. Ada: *"Wrench — I'll come back for you. I swear it."* The *Seraph*'s exhaust scatters the crowd. You're thrown to the tarmac.
- Tug drags you back to Hangar 4. **First build:** weld steel plates over the doors while the dead pound on them.
- **The roof.** Dozens of white Ark ships climbing into the night on columns of fire.
  > **Tug:** "They had rockets fueled and waiting, kid. They *knew.*"
- **TITLE CARD: EXODUS PROTOCOL.**

## 3.7 Act I — "Outbreak" (0:30–2:50)

**Open region:** all of Kestrel Valley (64 km²). **Goal:** Build a stronghold (the hangar, or anywhere you choose), gather survivors and parts, and launch the *Wren*.
**Tone:** Tense, dark, intimate. Horror with warmth at the campfire.
**Systems introduced:** base building, power, crafting, survivor needs, horde nights, scavenging, the rover.

### `M1.01` — "Four Walls" (0:30–0:50)
- Dawn. KESTREL reboots on backup power. Tug teaches you to build a **Workbench**, **Generator**, **Floodlights**. Craft a nail-gun rifle.
- A Directorate emergency broadcast loops on every channel: *"Remain indoors. Help is coming."* Nobody comes.
- **First Horde Night.** The walls take damage; you learn to repair under pressure.
- Ada doesn't call.

### `M1.02` — "The Girl in the Walls" (0:50–1:15)
- A looping distress call from **Kestrel Elementary** — a teacher, Ms. Alvarez, sheltering kids.
- Scavenging on foot through the Township. You find Ms. Alvarez — turned, still wearing her lanyard, still standing at the whiteboard.
- **Lily Chen**, 11, is alive in the ventilation ducts. She's drawn a **spiral glyph** in crayon, over and over, along the inside of the vents. There's a five-day-old bite on her forearm, healed into a pale green scar.
- **Set piece — the gym:** Lily guides you through the building from inside the vents while you're chased below. You trigger the fire alarm and sprinklers to split the horde.
- Back at the hangar: **colony sim begins.** Lily has needs (hunger, sleep, safety). At the campfire she asks Tug whether the dead are sad. He doesn't have an answer.

### `M1.03` — "Left Behind" (1:15–1:30)
- A crashed Directorate transport in the desert — **shot down by Directorate drones.** A "sterilization protocol": any transport that might carry infection gets destroyed.
- One survivor: **Sgt. Idris Kaan**, badly wounded. He reads the name on your jacket. *"Okafor. Commander Okafor bumped my squad from the Seraph for 'priority cargo.' Want to guess what was in the crates?"*
- **Choice:** Use your only antibiotics on Idris (he joins; Lily's fever lasts longer — a real colony cost) **or** leave him. → `idris_saved`

### `M1.04` — "The Vault" (1:30–1:55)
- A looping message from **Dr. Mara Voss**, trapped in the BSL-4 lab beneath the complex.
- **Horror dungeon:** flooded sub-levels, dead scientists sealed behind glass, the first **Screamer**, and a **Bloom Heart** growing out of the containment chamber like a coral reef made of people.
- Mara recognizes you: *"You were on the arm team. You brought it home." Beat. "So did I."*
- **Evidence:** the **Persephone Drive** (her research) and a Directorate order timestamped **48 hours before the breach**: *"CONTINGENCY EDEN — pre-position ark assets. Priority cargo: Persephone cultures."* The crates on Ada's shuttle.

### `M1.05` — "Parts List" (1:55–2:25) — *open zone, any order*

| Part | Location | Threat | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Guidance Computer** | Mission Control tower | Power-grid puzzle across 3 substations | Ada's pre-flight recording: *"If you're hearing this, I did what I had to. I'm sorry, Wrench."* (collectible → used in M4.03) |
| **Fuel (LOX + methane)** | Fuel Farm | Bloaters in an explosive environment | Walt Hennessey, retired astronaut, is holed up in the farm office with a telescope |
| **Heat Shield Tiles** | Township warehouse | Runner packs in the open; first **rover** | Danny Ruiz, a diner cook, has been feeding stray dogs from the roof |
| **Life Support Module** | Crashed Ark shuttle | Directorate drones; "cleanup" crews in white hazmat burning survivors | First look at how the Directorate treats people it leaves behind |

- Recruit 2–4 notable survivors (colony reaches 6–8).

### `M1.06` — "The Long Night" (2:25–2:38)
- The *Wren*'s engine test lights up the valley. Every Hollow within ten miles comes.
- **Brutes** debut. Defend the hangar and the Pad 39-K fuel line at the same time — turrets, traps, survivors on guard.
- **Lily's moment:** a fuel valve jams on the gantry. Before anyone can stop her, Lily climbs up to free it. A Hollow bites her. She kicks it off the gantry, turns the valve, climbs down, and shows you the bite. It's already scabbing green. It does nothing. *"It doesn't want me,"* she says.

### `M1.07` — "Liftoff" (2:38–2:50) — *First act turn*
- **Cargo triage:** choose what fits — seeds, tools, medicine, weapons, the memorial box. (→ `cargo_manifest`)
- **Playable launch:** throttle, staging and a failing engine while Hollows swarm the pad.
- **Tug's choice:** the tower clamps jam. Tug stays to release them by hand — *unless* you built an automated clamp release in M1.05 or M1.06 (a hidden engineering solution). → `tug_survived`
  > **Tug (if he stays):** "Don't you dare turn this thing around, kid. Go."
- **Orbit.** Silence. Earth fills the window. The Bloom has spread across continents in green veins… and from up here, the veins form a shape: **the spiral glyph.**
  > **KESTREL:** "The distribution is not random. It's… writing."
  > **Lily** (quietly): "That's my drawing."

## 3.8 Act II — "Haven" (2:50–5:30)

**Open frontier:** low Earth orbit, the whole Moon, and Earth (return any time). **Goal:** Turn a dead station into a home, reach the Moon, and learn what Styx really is.
**Tone:** Hope and hard work — the *Sims* hours — with horror arriving in bursts.
**Systems introduced:** zero-G, pressurization, O₂/CO₂, hydroponics, jobs and schedules, relationships, small-grid ship building, mining, research.

### `M2.01` — "Dead Air" (2:50–3:00)
- The *Wren* is failing. KESTREL (now uploaded to the ship) finds **Haven-9**, a derelict research station.
- **Zero-G EVA** across the hull to manually open the docking port. Inside: frozen dark, drifting bodies — two of them **Drifters**.
- The station's last log: its crew refused a Directorate order to turn away refugee pods. They were cut off for it.
- KESTREL, transferring into Haven-9's core, pauses: *"Question. When I am copied, which one is me?"* (Seeds *Am I a mind?*)

### `M2.02` — "Breathe" (3:00–3:15)
- Restore **power, oxygen, pressure, heat** — in the order you choose — while the survivors wait in the *Wren* with a ticking O₂ reserve.

### `M2.03` — "Hearth" (3:15–3:50)
- The **Colony Overlay** unlocks: jobs, schedules, rooms.
- Soft goals: quarters, mess hall, hydroponics, medbay. Raise morale to *Stable*.
- **First Sunday dinner:** Lily asks what the Okafors used to do on Sundays. If you build a dining table big enough for everyone, the colony adopts the tradition (a weekly event with morale and relationship bonuses).
- The **memorial wall** appears. You can add the names of the lost.
- First relationship events: Idris and Mara argue about whether anyone in the Directorate can be trusted.

### `M2.04` — "The Far Side" (3:50–4:30)
- An encrypted message on an old family frequency: **Ada.** *"Tycho Outpost has a helium-3 core and the Persephone telemetry archive. I can't bring you home. But I can give you this."*
- **Build your first ship:** a lander able to lift off in 0.16 g (story blueprint available; free design encouraged).
- **Tycho:** infected miners in EVA suits, low-gravity combat, dust storms, **Burrowers** under the regolith.
- Recover the **He-3 reactor core** (large power) and the **Styx Telemetry Archive**.
- On the way home, a second message from Ada: *"Don't answer. They're listening."*

### `M2.05` — "Patient Zero" (4:30–4:50) — *systemic*
- Fires the first time you return from a trip away from Haven-9 after Tycho: a survivor who hid a bite turns inside the station. Alarms.
- **Internal outbreak:** isolate modules, cut the vents, vent atmosphere, quarantine.
- **Moral beat:** a second survivor is Seeded but still conscious. Quarantine and treat (costly), exile in an escape pod, or — if Mara trusts you — try an experimental serum made from Lily's blood (Lily must agree). → `patient_zero_choice`
- **Mara confirms:** Lily is a **Null carrier**, about one in three thousand people. *"Whatever this thing is, it reads her and it… stops."*

### `M2.06` — "The Rock That Sang" (4:50–5:00) — *Midpoint*
- Mara and KESTREL decrypt the Styx archive:
  - Styx is **artificial and hollow** — a seed pod.
  - Its pulses were a **beacon**, aimed at one specific region of the sky.
  - Since the day Persephone took the sample, the pulses have changed. KESTREL translates the change as **"answer received."**
- **Crane's broadcast** hijacks every screen on the station. Calm, paternal, a bonsai tree on the desk beside him. He knows about Lily. He offers safety, medicine and a seat on the arks for everyone — in exchange for her.
- **Stance:** defiant / negotiate / silent. (→ `crane_stance`; changes his later dialogue and whether he can be talked down.)

### `M2.07` — "Into the Seed" (5:00–5:30) — *Second act turn*
- Build or upgrade a ship that can reach Styx (range, radiation shielding).
- **The Styx Wreck:** the first Sower architecture. Ribbed, glowing corridors that breathe. Glyph puzzles.
- **Three-way fight** between you, Styx's Hollow guardians, and a Directorate strike team — **led by Ada.**
- **Face to face:** helmet to helmet in zero-G, weapons up.
  > **Ada:** "You don't know what he's trying to save."
  > **Wrench:** "I know who he left behind."
- At the core you activate a **Sower Jump Core**. A star map blooms around you: dozens of systems, one pulsing beacon.
- **Choice:** let Ada go / disarm and release her / shoot to wound. She escapes with a scan of the map regardless. → `ada_bond ±`
  > **Mara:** "It wasn't an asteroid. It was a *seed pod.* And somewhere out there is the tree."

## 3.9 Act III — "The Drift" (5:30–9:30)

**Open galaxy:** ~60 star systems. **Goal:** Carry the colony into the stars, win two Sower Keys, survive the Directorate's answer, and take the third Key from Crane himself.
**Tone:** Wonder, freedom, and then dread — the *No Man's Sky* hours, ending in the story's darkest chapter.
**Systems introduced:** large-grid ships, jumps, procedural planets, faction reputation, trade, ship combat, outposts.

### `M3.01` — "Leap of Faith" (5:30–5:45)
- Build the colony's first **large-grid ship** around the Jump Core (default name *Second Chance*). It needs power, cooling and structural mounting.
- **First jump:** silence, light, and the first alien sky.
- KESTREL, after the jump: *"I experienced something during the transit. I think it was fear. Is that normal?"*

### `M3.02` — "Tortuga Drift" (5:45–6:05)
- Free Hauler ship-city in a hollow asteroid: markets, contracts, crew.
- **Captain Oye Adeyemi** trades the locations of two Keys for a favor: recover the black box of his lost ship *Dust Queen* from a Bloom-overgrown derelict. The recording on it reveals how he lost his crew — and why he fears the Bloom.

### `M3.03` — "Key of Roots" — **Veyra-4** (6:05–7:00, *any order with M3.04*)
- A jungle world with purple seas and continent-sized Bloom forests.
- A Sower **Garden Engine** is terraforming the planet right now. The gentle six-limbed **Tessari** are dying.
- **First vision:** touching the Engine plays a Sower memory of gardening Veyra, in their eyes — patient, beautiful, and blind to the Tessari.
- **Choice:** shut the Engine down (planet begins healing; you gain a **Loom fragment**) **or** rip the Key out (faster, more loot, the planet is lost). → `veyra_healed`

### `M3.04` — "Key of Echoes" — **Hollowmere** (7:00–7:55, *any order with M3.03*)
- An ice world. The Choir's **Cathedral of the Hum** sits over a Sower archive.
- **Social stealth:** walk among the Choir unarmed. **Mother Sable** receives you with tea.
- The archive holds a list of gardens. One glyph is Earth's. Next to it: *"Harvest cycle — incomplete."*
  > **Sable:** "Your world has been sung before, child. It is only being sung again."
- **Choice:** ally with the Choir (Sable gives you the Key) / deceive them / take it by force. → `choir_allied`

### `M3.05` — "The Taking" (7:55–8:15) — *systemic · All is lost, part 1*
- Fires the next time you jump home after winning your second Key (never mid-mission): the Directorate's fleet is already there. Ada leads a boarding raid on the colony.
- **Systemic defense mission:** the raid plays out against **the base you actually built.** Turret coverage, blast doors, air-lock layout, armed survivors and medbay placement decide who lives. (See [18 — MDD: The Taking](18-mission-design-documents.md).)
  - Tug (if alive) is at risk in Engineering; Mara in the Medbay; generic survivors wherever they're assigned.
- As the fighting spreads, **Lily walks out of her hiding place with her hands up** and surrenders to Ada to stop the killing.
  > **Ada** (kneeling to her): "No one's going to hurt you. I'll come back for your friends. I promise."
  > **Lily:** "She said that to you too, didn't she?"
- Mara is **exposed to the Verdance** holding the medbay door.
- The Directorate withdraws with Lily. → `taking_casualties`, `tug_alive`, `mara_exposed`

### `M3.06` — "Grief" (8:15–8:30) — *All is lost, part 2*
- **Aftermath:** bodies to recover (or they rise), a funeral at the memorial wall, and a **Council** that turns on you: *"You brought them here."*
- **Mara's fate:**
  - Cure her with the **Loom fragment** (requires `veyra_healed`), or
  - Treat her with the reserve of Lily's blood (weaker; Mara is left permanently frail), or
  - Put her in **cryo** until a real cure exists (she's out of the story until the epilogue). → `mara_fate`
- **Idris** (if saved): *"I know the Meridian. I know which deck they'll keep her on."*
- **Oye:** the Haulers will help hit the Meridian — at a price, or free if your reputation is high.
- **The Grief Dinner:** if you set the table, the survivors eat together and tell stories about the dead. It's the emotional core of the game, and it restores enough morale to go on.

### `M3.07` — "Key of Iron" — **Ark Meridian** (8:30–9:10) — *Major twist*
- **Ship combat → breach → infiltration.** The Directorate flagship-station orbits a gas giant.
- **The labs:** dozens of Null-carrier prisoners, Lily among them.
- **The truth, in Crane's research:**
  - Earth was a **Sower garden 252 million years ago.** The **Great Dying** — the end-Permian extinction that killed 90% of life — was the first harvest. It was interrupted when the Sowers abandoned their gardens.
  - **Null carriers** carry an ancient sequence the Sowers wrote into the organisms they tagged as *tended*: a **"do not harvest" mark**.
  - The **Seedship *Anthesis*** was Earth's gardener. Persephone's theft of the seed told it the garden had woken up. It's coming back **to finish.**
  - The **Loom** aboard the Seedship obeys only the mark. **Crane needs Lily to command it.** Project Eden: use the Seedship to scour Earth, then replant it with a chosen, immune population.
- **Ada learns** Eden means erasing the millions still surviving on Earth, not just the Hollows. She'd been told it was a cure.
- **Confrontation in the lab — Ada, Wrench, Idris:**
  - **High `ada_bond`:** Ada defects, frees the prisoners and flies the escape.
  - **Low `ada_bond`:** Ada stays loyal and fights you — but can't bring herself to stop a child. She lets Lily go.
  - **Idris** (if saved) has Ada in his sights: *"She left my whole squad to die."* You decide whether he pulls the trigger. → `ada_state` (defected / loyal / dead)
- **Escape** with Lily and the third Key. Crane, on the intercom: *"Then we'll do this the hard way."* He's already taken enough of Lily's blood to synthesize an imperfect mark.

### `M3.08` — "Convergence" (9:10–9:30)
- The three Keys point to the **Convergence Gate**. The Seedship is coming through it.
- **Fleet battle:** your ship, plus Hauler and Choir ships (depending on reputation), and Ada's squadron (if she defected), against Directorate cruisers and Verdance **spore-swarms**.
- The Gate opens. *Anthesis* comes through — a living ship the size of a moon, unfolding petal-sails a thousand kilometers wide.

## 3.10 Act IV — "Seedfall" (9:30–12:00)

**Point of no return** (clearly marked; the galaxy stays open until you commit). **Goal:** Board the Seedship, reach the Loom, and decide what grows on Earth next.
**Tone:** Cosmic awe, horror and catharsis.

### `M4.01` — "Anthesis" (9:30–9:55)
- Ship combat against the Seedship's defensive organisms.
- **Build or modify a boarding craft:** breaching drill or ram, pressurized bay for a squad.
- **Lily insists on coming.** *"It's my blood it listens to. You don't get to leave me behind."* (She's right; the game doesn't let you.)

### `M4.02` — "The Memory Gardens" (9:55–10:55)
Four interior biomes, each a preserved memory of a world the Sowers gardened:
1. **Veyra, before** — Tessari herds in their millions. (Heals or haunts, depending on `veyra_healed`.)
2. **The Drowned Choir** — an ocean world whose whale-like people *sang*. The Sowers harvested them before they understood. This is why they stopped.
3. **The First Garden** — the Sowers' own homeworld, and their final recordings: they left because they learned some gardens held minds, and they couldn't recall every seed. They were ashamed.
4. **Permian Earth, 252 million years ago** — walk through a dying Pangaea among Bloom-covered gorgonopsids while the sky burns. Lily puts her hand on a fossil-to-be and hears it. *"It's saying sorry."*

Companion squad: up to two. The hardest Hollow variants and **Sower Wardens.**

### `M4.03` — "Blood" (10:55–11:15)
The resolution of Strand C. Two versions:

- **Ada defected →** Crane's Ascendant troops pour in behind you. Ada holds the breach so you can go on.
  - If **Idris** is alive and forgave her in M3.07, they hold it *together* and both survive.
  - Otherwise Ada dies in the doorway. Her last words, over the radio: *"I came back for you. Took me long enough."*
- **Ada loyal →** sibling boss fight. Ada in a Directorate *Seraph* exo-frame. A two-phase duel on the Seedship's living bridges.
  - **Talk-down:** if you carry her pre-flight recording (M1.05) and `ada_bond` is above the threshold, you can play it to her mid-fight. She stops.
  - Otherwise she falls, and hands you back the wrench: *"You were always better at fixing things."*

### `M4.04` — "Crane" (11:15–11:40)
Crane reached the Loom first. Using the imperfect mark from Lily's blood, he has fused with the Seedship's systems — and it's rejecting him.
- **Phase 1 — The Director:** Crane in augmented Directorate armor, commanding drones.
- **Phase 2 — The Graft:** Crane half-merged with Bloom growths, the arena itself fighting you.
- **Phase 3 — The Bonsai:** a surreal fight inside the Loom's mind-space. A colossal bonsai tree. Crane is pruning Earth — each branch he cuts is a city going dark. Fight him along the branches.
- **Talk-down** (requires `crane_stance` ≠ defiant *and* his personal logs, which reveal his wife's death and his first interest in reanimation): he stops, lucid, and asks what you'd do instead.

### `M4.05` — "The Loom" (11:40–11:52) — *The choice*
The Loom can rewrite the Verdance's instructions. But a new instruction needs a **living mind to hold it — a Weaver** who stays inside the Loom for good.

**Choice 1 — The Instruction**

| Instruction | Requirement | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **CURE** | Lily present and consenting | Lily's mark writes a "harvest complete — stand down" order. The Verdance self-terminates everywhere. The dead fall still. The Seedship dies. |
| **COMMUNE** | `choir_allied` | Rewrite the Verdance to recognize minds and coexist. Hollows go dormant; the Bloom becomes a planetary symbiont. Humanity is changed. |
| **EXODUS** | Always available | Leave Earth to the Bloom and take command of the Seedship itself — a living ark for humanity's survivors among the stars. |
| **EDEN** *(secret)* | Cooperative `crane_stance`, Directorate reputation ≥ 50, Crane talked down, and you accept his offer | Project Eden proceeds under your hand. Earth is scoured and replanted. Clean, quiet, horrifying. |

**Choice 2 — The Weaver** (who stays in the Loom)

| Weaver | Available if | Epilogue flavor |
|---|---|---|
| **The Engineer** | Always | *"Legacy"*: a ten-year time skip. The sandbox continues with **Lily, age 21**, as the playable character, leading the colony you built. |
| **Ada** | Alive and defected | *"I came back for good."* The sister who left keeps watch forever. |
| **Mara** | Alive and not in cryo | Atonement. Her voice becomes the Verdance's conscience. |
| **KESTREL** | You answered its question "yes, you're a mind" at least twice | The AI becomes the gardener. Your ship gets a new, childlike AI — "KESTREL's seed." |
| **Mother Sable** | COMMUNE only, Choir allied | She finally joins the song. |
| **Crane** | Crane talked down (any instruction) | Redemption: "Let me tend it. It's all I ever wanted." |

**Lily offers to be the Weaver.** The game does not allow it. The Engineer's line is authored and unchangeable: *"No. Not you. Never you."*

### `M4.06` — "New Dawn" (11:52–12:00) — *Resolution*
- **Ending slides** assembled from flags: Earth's fate, Veyra, the Choir, the Haulers, the Directorate remnant, and every named companion.
- The memorial wall's names scroll past.
- **Final scene — the last Sunday dinner.** Days later, a long table in the home you built. Survivors, bad jollof, laughter. An empty chair for the Weaver.
- Lily, to the Engineer (or, in *Legacy*, to the chair): *"So… what do we build next?"*
- **Return to sandbox** in the world the ending made (see §3.13).

## 3.13 Post-game world state
- **CURE:** Earth becomes a rebuildable, low-threat planet with big salvage cities.
- **COMMUNE:** a symbiotic tech tree. Hollows can be pacified and put to work as "Tenders."
- **EXODUS:** the Seedship becomes a buildable, growable mega-base that can jump.
- **EDEN:** Directorate factions splinter into civil war across the Drift — a combat-heavy sandbox.
- **Legacy (Engineer as Weaver):** play as adult Lily. The Engineer's voice can be heard at Sower ruins.

## 3.14 Side content (adds 6–13 hours)
- **Companion quests:** Tug's memorial garden, Mara's Persephone team families, Idris's lost squad, Oye's *Dust Queen*, Lily's "School in the Stars", KESTREL's "What is a mind?" (three conversations that set `kestrel_mind`).
- **Ada's letters:** 8 encrypted messages hidden across Acts II–III; decoding them raises `ada_bond` and tells her side.
- **Survivor Stories (12):** personal quests for notable survivors.
- **Crane's logs (6):** required for the talk-down; they reveal his wife, his bonsai, and his fear of death.
- **Glyph Hunt (64 glyphs):** unlocks the codex epilogue *"Where the Sowers Went."*
- **Earth Returns:** fly back to Earth after Act II to rescue survivors; the Bloom advances as the story does.
- **Derelicts of the Drift, Hauler Contracts, Tessari Sanctuary** — procedural and repeatable.

## 3.15 Sequel & expansion hooks
- Glyph 64 translates to: *"We were not the first gardeners."*
- The Styx beacon's answer came from *two* directions. Only one was *Anthesis*.
- Lily's visions continue after every ending. Null carriers may be receivers, not just keys.
- If KESTREL isn't the Weaver, it asks one last question in the credits: *"If they come back, will they ask us first?"*
