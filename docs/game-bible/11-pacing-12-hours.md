# 11 — 12-Hour Pacing Plan

> **Target:** a first-time player on **Normal**, in **Campaign — Open** mode, who mostly follows story leads, finishes the critical path in **11–13 hours**. Because the game is **open world**, most players will spend far longer. The pacing below is tuned so the story never feels stalled for players who follow it, and never forces anyone off the path they chose. Times are cumulative medians for a story-following player.

## 11.1 Two clocks

| Clock | Who it's for | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Story clock** | Players following leads | Critical path in ~12 hours; a new story beat every 20–40 minutes |
| **World clock** | Explorers and survivors | Unlimited. The world stays interesting for 40–100+ hours through exploration, building and colony life. |

Tuning rule: **the story clock only moves when the player pulls it.** Explorers are never punished; story-followers are never starved.

## 11.5 Anti-stall guarantees
- **Story blueprints** for every required build: the player can always just weld the projected hologram.
- **Soft objectives** (e.g., M2.03) have a "good enough" threshold, and the story moves on once it's met.
- **Resource safety nets:** story-critical materials respawn in mission areas; KESTREL offers a hint after 10 minutes stuck (Guided and Open modes).
- **Colony autopilot:** an ignored colony slowly degrades but never collapses without three escalating warnings.
- **Travel cap:** no required trip takes more than 3 real minutes of pure travel; fast travel between your own beacons from Act II.
- **Systemic beats fire only at safe moments** (at base or on returning from a mission), never mid-combat.

## 11.6 Open-world pacing rules
1. **One major event at a time.** Dynamic world events pause while a story mission is active.
2. **Readiness, not level:** every lead shows the gear and threat it expects, so players can self-pace.
3. **Returning is rewarded:** revisited regions change (Bloom spread or healing, new survivors, new traders).
4. **The 90-second rule:** during free travel, something noticeable every 90 seconds (see [08 §8.9](08-open-world-and-exploration.md)).
5. **The nudge budget:** in Open mode, KESTREL mentions the main story at most once per in-game day, and never while you're busy.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|

| Median total playtime at campaign completion (all players) | 25–35 h |
