## 11.4 Anti-stall guarantees
- **Story blueprints** for every required build (the player can always "just weld the hologram").
- **Soft objectives** (M2.03) have a **"good enough" threshold** — the story advances once minimal needs are met.
- **Resource safety nets:** story-critical materials respawn in mission areas; KESTREL hints after 10 minutes stuck.
- **Colony auto-pilot:** if the player ignores the colony, it degrades slowly but never collapses without warning (3 escalating alerts first).
- **Travel time cap:** no required trip exceeds 3 real minutes of pure travel.
