## The pitch

Three years ago, your hands steered the robot arm that took a sample from an interstellar object. Tonight, that sample escapes the lab beneath your spaceport, and the dead rise. Your sister, a Directorate pilot, promises to hold you a seat on the last shuttle off Earth. Then she leaves you on the pad.

From there, the world is yours. Survive an open desert valley full of the dead. Fortify anywhere, rescue survivors, and build a rocket from salvage. In orbit, turn a dead station into a home full of people with names, needs and grudges. Build ships, land on the Moon, and jump into a procedural galaxy scarred by an ancient alien gardener. The plague was a tool for terraforming worlds, and Earth has been harvested once before. Now its Seedship is coming back to finish, the man who abandoned Earth wants to use the girl you saved to control it, and your sister is standing between you.

**Logline:** *A spaceport engineer who helped bring a plague to Earth must build a way off-world, protect a found family, and face the sister who left them behind, before the alien gardener that made the dead returns to finish its harvest.*

## One-page summary

**Cold Open — "Contact."** November 2068. You remote-operate the Persephone drill on the interstellar object Styx. Its surface flinches. Everyone cheers except the virologist in the back.

**Prologue — "Night Zero" (0:00–0:30).** March 24, 2071. A late shift in Hangar 4; a call from your sister Ada. Code Black. The dead rise. Ada holds a seat for you on Pad 12, then lifts off on Director Crane's order. *"I'll come back for you. I swear it."*

**Act I — "Outbreak" (open Kestrel Valley, to ~2:50).** Build a stronghold anywhere in a 64 km² desert valley. Rescue Lily, a girl the plague won't take; Idris, a soldier the Directorate shot down; and Mara, the scientist who knows what's happened. Find proof Crane knew 48 hours early. Build the *Wren* and launch. From orbit, the Bloom across Earth forms a glyph. *"It's writing."*

**Act II — "Haven" (open orbit and Moon, to ~5:30).** Bring a dead station back to life and turn it into a home. Sunday dinners. Build a lander from Ada's secret tip-off; loot the Moon. An outbreak inside the station. Lily is a Null carrier. Styx was a seed pod, and it has been answered. Meet Ada face to face at the Styx Wreck; win the Jump Core.

**Act III — "The Drift" (open galaxy, to ~9:30).** Build a capital ship and jump into ~60 procedural systems. Win two Sower Keys in any order: heal or plunder a jungle world, ally with or betray a cult on an ice world. Then the Directorate raids the home you built, and Lily surrenders to Ada to stop the killing. Grief. The rescue heist on the Ark Meridian reveals the twist: Earth was a Sower garden, the Great Dying was its first harvest, and Lily's blood carries the mark the Seedship obeys. Ada chooses a side.

**Act IV — "Seedfall" (to ~12:00).** Board the living Seedship *Anthesis*. Walk through the memories of the worlds it harvested, including Earth 252 million years ago. Face your sister, then Crane. At the Loom, choose what the Verdance becomes (**CURE / COMMUNE / EXODUS**, or the secret **EDEN**) and who stays behind to hold the new instruction. Then comes the last Sunday dinner: *"So… what do we build next?"* The open world continues in the state your ending created.

## Pillars (short form)
0. **Go anywhere, survive anything.** Open world, capability gates, a story that waits.
1. **Build what saves you.** Everything is placed by the player and physically simulated.
2. **People, not resources.** Survivors are characters; losing one should hurt.
3. **Fear on the ground, wonder in the sky.**
4. **A mystery worth crossing the galaxy for**, with a sister at its heart.
5. **Built to grow.** Data-driven everything; the campaign ends, the universe doesn't.
