# EXODUS PROTOCOL — Game Bible

> *"The dead didn't come from the ground. They came from the sky — and we brought them home."*

**Working title:** EXODUS PROTOCOL
**Studio:** ApesStudio
**Genre:** Open-world survival · engineering sandbox · colony life-sim · procedural space exploration
**Inspirations:** *Space Engineers* (block-based physical construction), *The Sims* (autonomous people with needs, moods and relationships), *No Man's Sky* (seamless procedural planets and space travel)
**Target platforms:** PC (primary), current-gen consoles (secondary)
**Players:** Single-player campaign; architecture ready for 2–4 player co-op
**Campaign length:** ~12 hours critical path (18–25 hours with side content), followed by an endless sandbox
**Rating target:** M / PEGI 16 (horror violence, no sexual content)

---

## Elevator pitch

A zombie outbreak tears through Earth. You are an engineer at a spaceport on the night it falls. Build defenses from scrap, rescue survivors, and hack together a rocket to escape to orbit. Up there you'll rebuild a dead station into a home, keep your people fed, sane and alive, and eventually build ships that jump between procedurally generated star systems — chasing the truth about where the plague came from, and whether Earth can still be saved.

## One-page summary

- **Act I — Outbreak (Earth, ~0:00–2:45).** Kestrel Spaceport, Nevada. The Persephone sample-return capsule landed three weeks ago. Tonight the dead rise. Survive, fortify a hangar, gather six survivors, and assemble a launch vehicle from parts.
- **Act II — Haven (Low Earth Orbit & Moon, ~2:45–5:30).** Dock with the derelict research station *Haven-9*. Restore power, air and gravity. Build a real colony. Mine the Moon. Learn that the Persephone sample was not a rock — it was a seed.
- **Act III — The Drift (Deep space, ~5:30–9:30).** Reverse-engineer a precursor jump core. Leap to procedural star systems, meet rival survivor factions, and find the ruins of the **Sowers** — the ancient intelligence that built the plague as a terraforming tool.
- **Act IV — Seedfall (~9:30–12:00).** A Sower *Seedship* is waking. Board it, reach its heart, and make the choice: **Cure** Earth, **Commune** with the Bloom, or lead humanity on an **Exodus** to the stars. The sandbox continues after credits.

## Core pillars (short form)

1. **Build what saves you.** Every wall, wire and thruster is placed by the player and physically simulated.
2. **People, not resources.** Survivors are characters with needs, opinions and relationships — losing one should hurt.
3. **Fear on the ground, wonder in the sky.** Planetside is tense horror-survival; space is awe and freedom.
4. **A mystery worth crossing the galaxy for.** Hand-authored story threaded through a procedural universe.
5. **Built to grow.** Every system is data-driven so new planets, blocks, enemies and story arcs can be added without rewrites.
