# 10 — Progression & Economy

## 10.1 Progression axes
1. **Tech tree** (research) — unlocks blocks, items, stations.
2. **Colony growth** — more survivors, more skills, more jobs.
3. **Player gear** — suits, weapons, multitool upgrades.
4. **Territory** — Earth → orbit → Moon → the Drift.
5. **Faction reputation** — trade, blueprints, allies.
6. **Story knowledge** — codex, glyphs, endings unlocked.

There is **no XP level for the player**. The Engineer grows through gear, tech and people — reinforcing "people, not resources."

### Research
- **Research Points (RP)** come from: scanning new things, analyzing samples in the lab, salvaging data cores, survivors working as Researchers.
- **Research types:** Engineering RP (blue), Bio RP (green), Precursor RP (teal).
- Tech tree is a **web**, not a line: players choose branches (e.g., Defense vs. Automation early in Act II), but story-critical nodes are always reachable.

## 10.4 Economy & trade
- **Currency:** none on Earth (barter). In the Drift, Free Haulers use **Credits** ("chits"), backed by fuel and medicine.
- **Barter value** of items depends on faction needs (Choir want medical supplies; Haulers want fuel and parts; Directorate won't trade unless reputation is high).
- **Contracts board** at Tortuga Drift: delivery, salvage, escort, bounty, rescue. Scaled to player tech tier.
- **Survivor-driven economy:** colony can produce trade goods (meals, art, medicine, refined metals) for export — gives non-combat players a progression path.

## 10.5 Faction reputation

| Faction | Scale | Gains | Losses | Rewards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free Haulers** | −100…+100 | Contracts, fair trades, rescuing haulers | Theft, attacking traders | Blueprints, crew, cheaper fuel, allies in M3.07 |
| **Choir** | −100…+100 | Listening, sharing Bloom samples, non-violence at Hollowmere | Killing Choir, burning shrines | Spore tech, Hollow-calming items, Commune ending |
| **Directorate** | −100…+100 | Handing over intel (morally grey), sparing officers | Raids, freeing prisoners | Access to Directorate tech, Crane talk-down, Eden ending |

## 10.6 Difficulty presets

| Setting | Story | Normal | Survivor | Hardcore |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vital drain | 50% | 100% | 125% | 150% |
| Enemy damage | 60% | 100% | 130% | 160% |
| Infection gain | 50% | 100% | 125% | 150%, no player resistance |
| Resources | ×2 | ×1 | ×0.75 | ×0.5 |
| Survivor death | Downed only | Permanent | Permanent | Permanent |
| Story immunity | On | On | On | Off |
| Structural collapse | Off | Simplified | Full | Full |
| Player death | Respawn | Respawn | Respawn + drop gear | **Permadeath** (save deleted) |

All sliders can also be adjusted individually in **Custom**.

## 10.7 Rewards cadence (12-hour path)
- **Every 10–15 minutes:** a new block/item or small upgrade.
- **Every ~45 minutes:** a new system or station unlock.
- **Every ~90 minutes:** a new region or biome.
- **Every act:** a new movement/travel mode (foot → rover → ship → jump).
