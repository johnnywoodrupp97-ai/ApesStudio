# 02 — World & Lore

## 2.1 Setting at a glance

- **When:** 2071. Near-future Earth. Commercial spaceflight is routine; a thin human presence exists in low Earth orbit and on the Moon.
- **Where it starts:** Kestrel Aerospace Complex, a private spaceport and research campus in the Nevada desert.
- **Where it goes:** Low Earth orbit → the Moon → a procedurally generated region of the galaxy called **the Drift**, reachable through precursor jump technology.
- **Tone:** Grounded near-future sci-fi (worn industrial hardware, NASA-punk) that slowly opens into cosmic wonder and cosmic horror.

## 2.2 Timeline

| Date | Event |

## 2.3 The pathogen: the Verdance

In-universe, survivors call it **"the Bloom"** or **"the Green."** Scientists call it **LZR-1 "Lazarus."** The Sowers called it something untranslatable, rendered in glyphs as **"the Gardener's Hand."** This bible uses **the Verdance** as the canonical term.

### What it is
- A self-replicating, fungus-like **biological nanomachine**. Neither virus nor fungus — a designed lifeform.
- Its purpose is **terraforming**: it converts a biosphere's biomass into a uniform, Sower-compatible ecology.
- It treats complex animals as **mobile biomass** — reanimating the dead to spread itself and to carry material to growing **Bloom Hearts**.

### How it spreads (drives gameplay)
| Vector | Gameplay expression |
|---|---|
| **Bites & scratches** from Hollows | Wound infection — fast, dangerous, treatable early |
| **Spores** near Bloom growth and Bloaters | Airborne exposure — needs filtration masks / sealed suits |
| **Contaminated water & food** | Must purify; spreads inside colonies if ignored |
| **Death** | Anyone who dies with Verdance in their system rises as a Hollow within minutes |

### Stages of infection (humans)
1. **Exposure** — asymptomatic, detectable only by scanner.
2. **Seeding** — fever, green veining, hunger; treatable with **Suppressant**.
3. **Bloom** — delirium, aggression, lichen-like growths. Only experimental treatment works.
4. **Turning** — death and reanimation as a Hollow.

### Why some people are immune (story hook)
Roughly 1 in 3,000 humans carries a mitochondrial variant the Verdance cannot read — **"Null carriers."** Lily Chen is one. The player is *partially* resistant (explains how they survive bites early in Act I with Suppressant). This is the key to the Cure ending.

### The Verdance is not evil
It is a tool without its wielders — a gardener that never got told to stop. Late-game revelation: it can be **re-instructed** by the Sower "Loom" aboard the Seedship. This reframes the whole conflict and sets up the three endings.

## 2.4 The Sowers (precursors)
- A civilization that uploaded itself into engineered biology and stellar-scale machinery. They did not see themselves as conquerors — they "gardened" worlds they believed were empty of minds.
- **Architecture:** grown rather than built — ribbed, coral-like spires; bioluminescent teal glyphs; chambers that "breathe."
- **Technology found in-game:** Jump Cores (FTL), Loom Nodes (genetic re-writers), Resonance Keys (data), Garden Engines (terraformers).
- **Where they went:** Deliberately left unresolved — the central hook for future expansions (see [14 — Expansion Roadmap](14-expansion-roadmap.md)).

## 2.5 Factions

### The Survivors (player faction — named by the player; default "the Kestrel Remnant")
Engineers, scientists, soldiers and civilians. Pragmatic, frightened, stubbornly hopeful.

### The Ark Directorate
- Military-corporate elite that escaped to orbit in armed ark ships on Night Zero.
- Led by **Director Halvard Crane**. Holds the orbital high ground and the best technology.
- Goal: control the Verdance, harvest immunity from Null carriers, and "reseed" Earth with a chosen population.
- **Aesthetic:** clean white-and-gold hulls, drones, sterile propaganda.
- **Role:** primary human antagonist; can be negotiated with in part.

### The Choir
- A cult born in the first weeks, convinced the Bloom is a rapture. Members deliberately infect themselves and use Suppressant to hover in Stage 2 ("the Hum"), claiming to hear the Verdance "sing."
- Led by **Mother Sable**. Unsettlingly kind. Surprisingly, partly right — they *can* sense Sower signals.
- **Aesthetic:** hand-stitched robes over EVA suits, living moss armor, candles in helmets.
- **Role:** wild card; ally or enemy depending on choices. Key to the Commune ending.

### The Free Haulers
- Asteroid miners, orbital truckers and spacers stranded off-world at the outbreak. Independent, mercenary, practical.
- Led loosely by **Captain Oyelaran "Oye" Adeyemi** from the ship-city *Tortuga Drift*.
- **Aesthetic:** patched-together ships, graffiti, cargo-container habitats.
- **Role:** primary traders; reputation unlocks blueprints and crew.

### The Hollow (not a faction — an ecology)
All reanimated creatures, human and animal, directed by the Verdance's distributed "instinct." Coordinated near Bloom Hearts, feral elsewhere.

## 2.7 Environmental storytelling rules
1. **Every derelict tells a story in three objects** (e.g., a barricade, a child's drawing, an empty Suppressant box).
2. **Logs are short** — under 45 seconds of audio or 120 words of text.
3. **The Bloom grows over time** — revisited places become greener, more hostile; cleared places visibly recover.
4. **Sower glyphs are a learnable language.** Each translated glyph unlocks a codex entry; completing sets reveals optional lore endings.
