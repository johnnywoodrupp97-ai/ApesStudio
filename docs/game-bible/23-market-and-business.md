# 23 — Market, Business & Launch

## 23.1 Market position

```
            STORY DEPTH ▲
                        │                         ★ EXODUS PROTOCOL
       Story-led        │   The Last of Us        (AAA story + deep
       action/horror    │   Dead Space             open-world sandbox)
                        │
                        │   Subnautica  Frostpunk
                        │
                        │   No Man's Sky          Starfield
                        │
       Pure             │   7 Days to Die  RimWorld   The Sims
       sandbox          │   Space Engineers   Valheim
                        └────────────────────────────────────────▶ SYSTEMIC / SANDBOX DEPTH
```

**The gap we fill:** open-world survival-builders have deep systems but thin stories. Story-led games have strong characters but little freedom or building. Nobody combines **physical engineering, a real colony life-sim, open planetary exploration and an AAA-quality character story** in one game.

## 23.2 Competitive comparison

| Game | What players love | What's missing that we offer |
|---|---|---|
| **Space Engineers** | Physical building, ships, engineering freedom | Story, characters, people to build *for* |
| **No Man's Sky** | Seamless planets, wonder, scale | Weight and consequence; a story with emotional stakes |
| **The Sims** | People with lives; attachment | Survival stakes; first-person presence; a world outside the house |
| **7 Days to Die** | Base defense, hordes, crafting | Space, a colony that matters, narrative |
| **Subnautica** | Exploration, mystery, dread | Scale beyond one planet; a colony; co-op (planned) |
| **RimWorld** | Emergent colony drama | First-person presence, 3D building, exploration |
| **The Last of Us** | Characters and grief | Freedom, building, systemic play |

## 23.4 Business model
- **Premium, one-time purchase.** No loot boxes, no pay-to-win, no battle pass, no timed FOMO.
- **Pricing (proposal):**

| SKU | Price (USD) | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Early Access (optional, PC) | $29.99 | Prologue + Acts I–II + Survival Sandbox; price rises at 1.0 |
| Standard (1.0) | $49.99 | Full game |
| Deluxe | $69.99 | Full game + Expansion 1 + a cosmetic block set + digital artbook & soundtrack |
| Expansions | $19.99–$29.99 | Story + region + systems (see [14](14-expansion-roadmap.md)) |
| Cosmetic packs (optional) | $4.99–$9.99 | Block skins, suit styles. Never gameplay advantages. |

- **Free updates** carry systems (co-op, colony depth, mod tools); **paid expansions** carry story and regions.
- **Ethics statement:** everything that affects gameplay can be earned in play. Blueprints shared by players are always free.

## 23.5 Platforms & release plan
1. **PC (Steam, Epic, GOG)** — lead platform; Steam Deck verified target.
2. **PlayStation 5 & Xbox Series X|S** — at 1.0, with controller parity from day one of development.
3. **Optional Early Access** on PC once Acts I–II are content-complete (see [15](15-production-plan.md)).

## 23.7 Community & UGC
- **Blueprint sharing** at launch (in-game browser + Steam Workshop).
- **Mod tools** and the **scenario editor** in the "Workshop" free update.
- **Monthly build challenges** with in-game cosmetic rewards.
- **Official Discord**, with a public roadmap and patch notes.

## 23.9 Ratings & compliance
- **Target:** ESRB **M** (Blood and Gore, Violence, Strong Language) · PEGI **16** (18 possible for body horror; the *body-horror reduction* toggle is not rating-relevant).
- No real-money gambling mechanics; no loot boxes (compliant in BE/NL and other strict regions).
- Child-character guidelines (see [17 §17.8](17-narrative-design.md)) reviewed with ratings consultants early.
- Privacy: telemetry is opt-in, anonymized, and GDPR/CCPA compliant.
