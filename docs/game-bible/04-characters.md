# 04 — Characters

> Visual design, wardrobe by act and Blender production specs for every character are in the **[Character Bible](../character-bible/README.md)**.

## 4.1 Character writing principles
1. **Everyone is tired.** Nobody has slept properly in weeks. Humor is gallows humor.
2. **Nobody is purely right.** Crane has a logic, Sable has a truth, Ada has a debt, Mara has a guilt.
3. **Companions are also colonists.** Story characters live in the colony sim with needs and moods, but have **story immunity** except at authored moments and in systemic story beats (The Taking). *Hardcore Story* removes the immunity.
4. **Show, don't tell.** Relationships move through shared meals, work, gifts and small barks — not only cutscenes.
5. **Every major character has a Want, a Need, a Wound and a Lie** (below). Their arc is the Lie breaking.

## 4.2 The Player — "Wrench" (the Engineer)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Default name** | Ren Okafor. First name, appearance, voice (3 sets) and pronouns are customizable. The surname is fixed: the family tie to Ada. |
| **Nickname** | **"Wrench"** — given by Ada when you were kids. Every VO line uses it, so custom names never break the voice acting. |
| **Age / role** | Early 30s. Senior systems engineer on the *Wren* program; three years ago, a junior operator on the Persephone sample arm. |
| **Want** | Get the people in front of them to safety. |
| **Need** | To accept responsibility for what they helped start, without drowning in it. |
| **Wound** | Raised by Ada after their mother died; Ada chose her career over them once before. |
| **Lie** | *"I just fix machines. The big decisions belong to someone else."* |
| **Arc** | Act I: protect. Act II: lead. Act III: grieve and choose. Act IV: decide for a species. |

**Background (character creation)** — a starting perk and a dialogue flavor:

| Background | Perk | Dialogue flavor |
|---|---|---|
| **Flight Engineer** | +20% build speed; starts with a welder upgrade | Technical problem-solver |
| **Former Army Medic** | Suppressant 25% more effective; starts with a medkit | Calm, caring |
| **Ex-Test Pilot** | +10% thrust efficiency on ships you fly | Cocky, dry |
| **Colony Planner** | +10% survivor mood in rooms you built | Diplomatic, visionary |

- **Partial resistance:** the Engineer isn't a Null carrier, but infection progresses 3× slower once Seeded. Mara explains it in Act II as a lucky, partial match to the mark. It's why a new player survives early mistakes (off on Hardcore).
- **Dialogue:** short, tone-tagged choices — **Direct / Warm / Pragmatic / Wry**. No morality meter; each companion reacts in their own way.

## 4.3 Family

### Commander Ada Okafor — *The Sister* (Strand C)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Age / role** | 38. Directorate flight commander, pilot of the shuttle *Seraph*; Crane's protégée. |
| **Want** | To save as many people as possible, the way Crane says it can be done. |
| **Need** | To stop following orders and choose who she is. |
| **Wound** | When their mother was dying of a degenerative disease, Crane paid for her experimental treatment. It bought eight months. Ada has owed him ever since. |
| **Lie** | *"Someone has to make the hard calls. If I obey, the blood isn't on me."* |
| **Look** | Immaculate white-and-gold flight suit; a tired face; a tattoo of her mother's handwriting on her wrist: *Sunday.* |
| **Voice** | Clipped, controlled; slips into the family's old warmth when she's tired. |

**Arc by act:**

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Promises to hold a seat for Wrench, then leaves on Crane's order. |
| I | Silent. Only her pre-flight recording, found in Mission Control. |
| II | Secret messages (Tycho). Leads the Directorate team at Styx. Face to face for the first time. |
| III | Leads The Taking; promises Lily no one will be hurt. On the Meridian she learns what Eden really is and **defects or doesn't**, depending on `ada_bond`. |
| IV | Holds the breach ("I came back for you") **or** is the sibling boss. Can be the Weaver. |

**`ada_bond` (−100 to +100):** raised by answering her messages, sparing her at Styx, decoding her 8 letters, and choosing *Warm* in key conversations; lowered by *Direct* hostility, harming Directorate pilots, or letting Idris shoot. Threshold for defection: **+40**.

**Signature lines:**
- *"Pad 12. I'm holding a seat for you. Run."*
- *"You don't know what he's trying to save."*
- *"I came back for you. Took me long enough."*

## 4.5 Antagonists

### Director Halvard Crane — The Ark Directorate
| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 67. Former defense-industry CEO; head of the Directorate. |
| **Want** | To save humanity from itself by starting over with the "fit" and the immune. |
| **Wound** | His wife, Elena, died slowly of a degenerative disease. His first interest in Persephone was the reanimation effect. |
| **Lie** | *"A gardener who won't prune is just watching things die."* |
| **Motif** | A 90-year-old bonsai juniper on his desk that he prunes during every broadcast. |
| **Presence** | Mostly heard: broadcasts, propaganda, holo-messages. Calm and paternal. Appears in person only in Acts I (cold open) and IV. |
| **Boss** | M4.04, three phases. Can be talked down; can be the Weaver. |
| **Voice** | *"I didn't end the world. I simply refused to go down with it."* |

### Mother Sable — The Choir
| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 50s. Former hospice chaplain. Infected on Night Zero; kept at Stage 2 by Suppressant; hears the Verdance. |
| **Belief** | Death is a door. The Bloom is a song worth joining. |
| **Truth** | She's partly right: Null carriers and the Stage-2 infected really can sense Sower signals. |
| **Role** | Ally or enemy by your choice. Key to COMMUNE. Can be the Weaver. |
| **Voice** | *"You call them the dead. I call them the listening."* |

### The Verdance
A force of nature with an eerie, emergent intelligence. Hollows mimic human habits: knocking, repeating words, returning to their homes. In the Loom, KESTREL finally translates its "voice": not hate, only instruction.

## 4.6 Generic & notable survivors
- **Procedural survivors:** generated from name, portrait, backstory, trait and skill tables (see [07 — Colony Life-Sim](07-colony-life-sim.md)).

  - **Danny Ruiz** — diner cook; *Comfort Eater, Optimist*; runs the mess hall; wants to rebuild his grandmother's recipe book.
  - **Priya Nair** — air-traffic controller; *Perfectionist, Claustrophobic*; great at logistics, hates small rooms.
  - **Walt Hennessey** — retired astronaut, 74; *Frail, Storyteller*; boosts morale, teaches piloting, keeps a pre-2071 log about Styx.
  - **Aiyana Two Rivers** — park ranger; *Outdoorsy, Sharpshooter*; the best planetside scout.
  - **Marcus & Joel Webb** — a married couple; *Bonded*; if one dies, the other breaks.

## 4.8 Casting & performance notes
- **Performance capture** for all story companions and antagonists (face and body). Barks recorded in studio.
- **Ada and Wrench** must be cast together and chemistry-read as siblings; both voice sets for Wrench are recorded opposite the same Ada.
- **Lily:** cast an age-appropriate young actor with a qualified child-performer supervisor. Keep sessions short, and the darkest scenes are written so they never need to be performed graphically.
- **Crane:** warmth first. The audience should *want* to believe him in M2.06.
- **KESTREL:** no robotic filtering; a warm, precise human voice with deliberately slightly-too-even pacing.
