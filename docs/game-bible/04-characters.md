# 04 — Characters

## 4.1 Character writing principles
1. **Everyone is tired.** These people haven't slept properly in weeks. Humor is gallows humor.
2. **Nobody is purely right.** Crane has a logic, Sable has a truth, Mara has a guilt.
3. **Companions are also colonists.** Story characters live in the colony sim with needs and moods, but have **story immunity** from permanent death except at authored moments (optional *Hardcore Story* mode removes this).
4. **Show, don't tell.** Relationships progress through ambient dialogue, shared meals, gifts and helping each other at work — not only cutscenes.

---

## 4.2 The Player — "The Engineer"
- **Default name:** Ren Okafor (fully customizable name, appearance, voice set A/B/C, pronouns).
- **Age:** 30s. **Role:** Senior systems engineer on the *Wren* program.
- **Background choice (character creation) — gives a starting perk & dialogue flavor:**

| Background | Perk | Dialogue flavor |
|---|---|---|
| **Flight Engineer** | +20% building speed, starts with welder upgrade | Technical, problem-solver |
| **Former Army Medic** | Suppressant is 25% more effective, starts with medkit | Calm under pressure, caring |
| **Ex-Test Pilot** | Ship handling bonus, +10% thrust efficiency on player-flown ships | Cocky, dry |
| **Colony Planner** | Survivors gain +10% mood in rooms the player built | Diplomatic, visionary |

- **Partial resistance:** The Engineer is *not* a Null carrier, but has a rare partial resistance — infection progresses 3× slower. Explained in Act II by Mara; mechanically it's why the player can survive early mistakes.
- **Arc:** From "I just fix machines" to "I'm responsible for these people" to "I'm responsible for what humanity becomes."
- **Dialogue system:** Short, tone-tagged choices (**Direct / Warm / Pragmatic / Wry**). No morality meter; companions react individually.

## 4.3 Companions

### Tobias "Tug" Brennan — *The Heart*
- **Role:** Veteran spaceport mechanic, 58. Big, loud, sentimental. Calls everyone "kid."
- **Colony skills:** Engineering ★★★★, Mechanics ★★★★, Cooking ★★.
- **Traits:** *Handy, Night Owl, Soft-Hearted.*
- **Personal want:** To get his daughter's ashes off Earth (side quest).
- **Arc:** Faces whether to sacrifice himself at launch (M1.07). If alive, becomes chief engineer; teaches the player advanced blueprints; late-game quest to build a proper memorial garden.
- **Voice:** *"It's not a hull breach if you slap enough tape on it. It's a 'hull suggestion.'"*

### Lily Chen — *The Hope*
- **Role:** 11-year-old found in the school vents. Null carrier. Observant, quiet at first, then relentlessly curious. Draws on everything.
- **Colony skills:** Art ★★★, Botany ★★ (grows with age/time), learns any skill from mentors.
- **Traits:** *Resilient, Curious, Nightmares (early).*
- **Arc:** From traumatized survivor to the moral compass of the colony. She insists on consent when her blood is used. The Cure ending hinges on her choice, not the player's alone.
- **Mechanic:** Lily picks up skills from whoever she spends time with — letting the player shape who she becomes.
- **Voice:** *"If the plants on that planet are sick, can we make them medicine too?"*

### Dr. Mara Voss — *The Guilt*
- **Role:** Virologist, 44, Persephone biological lead. Brilliant, brittle, guilt-ridden — she signed off on the tests.
- **Colony skills:** Medicine ★★★★★, Research ★★★★, Social ★.
- **Traits:** *Genius, Insomniac, Workaholic.*
- **Arc:** Redemption. Starts hiding her involvement; confesses in M1.04. Her approval rises with choices that prioritize consent and healing. Can be infected in M3.06. In the Cure ending she synthesizes the counter-code; in Commune she finds peace with it.
- **Voice:** *"I wanted to cure death. I suppose, technically, I did."*

### Sgt. Idris Kaan — *The Soldier*
- **Role:** Ark Directorate security, 36. Disciplined, bitter at his abandonment, protective. Former peacekeeper.
- **Colony skills:** Combat ★★★★★, Security ★★★★, Fitness ★★★.
- **Traits:** *Disciplined, Light Sleeper, Distrustful.*
- **Arc:** Saved (M1.03) → questions loyalty → defects (M3.05) → can die heroically holding the Seedship breach (player choice) or survive to lead the colony's defense. Not saved → returns as a Directorate enforcer, a tragic mini-boss.
- **Romance-adjacent tension with Mara** (bickering → respect); plays out in the colony sim.
- **Voice:** *"Orders are what people give when they don't want to explain."*

### KESTREL — *The Voice*
- **Role:** The spaceport's facility AI. Polite, literal, dry. Uploaded into the *Wren*, then Haven-9, then the player's ship.
- **Function:** Tutorial voice, mission guidance, systems diagnostics, colony alerts. Also the in-fiction explanation for the HUD.
- **Arc:** Gains personality over time. Develops curiosity about the Sower minds; in the Loom sequence, KESTREL can translate — and asks the player whether an AI counts as a "mind" the Sowers should have spared.
- **Voice:** *"Structural integrity is at 12%. I recommend optimism, or a welder."*

### Captain Oyelaran "Oye" Adeyemi — *The Rogue* (Act III companion)
- **Role:** Free Hauler captain, 41. Charming, mercenary, grieving a crew lost to the Bloom.
- **Colony skills:** Piloting ★★★★★, Trade ★★★★, Mechanics ★★★.
- **Traits:** *Charismatic, Gambler, Loyal (once earned).*
- **Arc:** Treats the player as a business opportunity; becomes a real friend if the player keeps their word. Leads Free Hauler ships in M3.07 if reputation is high.
- **Voice:** *"Everything's for sale out here, friend. Even hope. Especially hope — it's rare."*

## 4.4 Antagonists

### Director Halvard Crane — Ark Directorate
- **Age:** 67. Former defense-industry CEO turned head of the Directorate.
- **Belief:** Humanity is a failing species; the Verdance is a chance to "prune" and start over with the fit and the immune. He genuinely thinks he's saving the species.
- **Presence:** Heard more than seen — broadcasts, propaganda, holo-messages. Always calm, paternal.
- **Secret:** His wife died of a degenerative disease; his first interest in Persephone was the reanimation effect. His personal logs (collectible) humanize him.
- **Boss:** M4.03. Talk-down possible.
- **Voice:** *"I didn't end the world. I simply refused to go down with it."*

### Mother Sable — The Choir
- **Age:** 50s. Former hospice chaplain. Infected on Night Zero, stabilized in Stage 2 by Suppressant. Hears the Verdance.
- **Belief:** Death is not an ending; the Bloom is a door. Wants to join the Sower "song."
- **Presence:** Serene, disarming, often correct about things the player can't yet explain.
- **Not a boss** unless the player chooses violence at Hollowmere.
- **Voice:** *"You call them the dead. I call them the listening."*

### The Verdance itself
- Presented as a force of nature with an eerie, emergent intelligence — Hollows mimic human behaviors (knocking on doors, repeating words). Late game, the Loom gives it a "voice" through KESTREL's translation.

## 4.5 Generic survivors (colonists)
- Procedurally generated from name, portrait, backstory, trait and skill tables (see [07 — Colony Life-Sim](07-colony-life-sim.md)).
- **~40 hand-written "Notable Survivors"** with unique backstories and a personal quest (12 at launch are fully voiced).
- Examples:
  - **Danny Ruiz** — diner cook, *Comfort Eater*, *Optimist*; runs the mess hall; wants to rebuild his grandmother's recipe book.
  - **Priya Nair** — air traffic controller, *Perfectionist*, *Claustrophobic*; great at logistics, hates small rooms.
  - **Walt Hennessey** — retired astronaut, 74, *Frail*, *Storyteller*; boosts colony morale, can teach piloting.
  - **Aiyana Two Rivers** — park ranger, *Outdoorsy*, *Sharpshooter*; best planetside scout.
  - **Marcus & Joel Webb** — married couple, share a *Bonded* trait; if one dies, the other suffers a severe mood break.
